## Spatial result and limits

Projecting a detector box bottom gives a floor **proxy**, not a verified shoe contact. A source [shoe review for #9](../data/processed/fqe_r1_boundary_recheck/p9_ground_proxy_review_sheet.jpg) shows the point sometimes between separated feet or shifting with stance. Her 15-frame-median proxy path is **8.9 m**; using 5–31-frame medians changes it to **10.6–8.3 m**. This method sensitivity is not a confidence interval or measured physical travel. #12's analogous gap-filled diagnostic varies from **20.2 to 13.0 m**; #20's four-frame-repaired diagnostic varies from **10.9 to 6.2 m**. Neither is reported as player distance, and no #13 distance is calculated.

A conservative 3 m court-grid diagnostic requires 5-, 15-, and 31-frame-smoothed projections to agree and each point to lie at least 0.6 m from internal zone lines. It assigns #9 **226 back-middle** and **21 back-right** frames, leaving **147/394 unresolved**. For #20 it assigns **79 middle-left** and **16 front-left**, leaving **269/364 unresolved**, including four truncated-box frames. These are single-reviewer development classifications; the margin is a review rule, not a measured error bound. They are too incomplete to stand in for full-team occupancy.

## Shoe-seeded tracking experiment

The user's proposed workflow starts with a visually identified player and shoe, then follows the shoe through later frames. A [seeded shoe candidate module](../services/inference/seeded_shoe_tracker.py) and [bounded #10 colour experiment](../data/processed/fqe_r1_boundary_recheck/r1_p10_color_shoe_experiment.json) test that idea on the 34-frame gap where her whole-player box disappears. The starting left pink shoe is an existing analyst source-point proposal at frame 21098. The colour search is centred on the last candidate, and its thresholds were chosen for this one pink shoe.

The colour method finds a candidate on **all 34 missing-box frames**; its [three review sheets](../data/processed/fqe_r1_boundary_recheck/r1_p10_color_shoe_sheet_1.jpg) place the markers on visible pink regions. A [fixed-image shoe template](../data/processed/fqe_r1_boundary_recheck/r1_p10_seeded_shoe_experiment.json) lasts only **11 of 36 frames** before foreground #9 obscures its appearance. The colour method also has a **23 px component-centre jump at frame 21115** when a partially hidden shoe becomes more visible; that jump is not a measured step. It finds no candidate at frame 21133, when the person box has returned but the pink region is obscured.

This supports a **shoe-first development path** for player continuity: identify the player with the full image at a clear seed frame, track each shoe as an appearance candidate, and use body/jersey evidence to recheck identity after overlaps. A colour blob centre is neither the sole-floor contact nor a body-centre position. Similar white shoes, two feet per player, jumping, hidden soles and camera perspective still need explicit handling. The experiment produces no court coordinate, distance or production tracking replacement.

A [fixed-size whole-player box check](../data/processed/fqe_r1_boundary_recheck/r1_p10_shoe_anchored_box_review.json) applies the user's proposed translation literally: keep #10's box dimensions from frame 21098 and move it with the pink-shoe colour centre.
