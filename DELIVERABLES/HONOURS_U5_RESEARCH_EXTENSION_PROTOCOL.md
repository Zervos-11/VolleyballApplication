# Rally, transfer and transition extension protocol

**Frozen on 6 November 2026 before new source-state review or transfer labels.** This is an extension of the completed research prototype, not a replacement for its original results. The [current S3 P34 baseline](HONOURS_U5_S3P34_WHOLE_INTERVAL_COVERAGE.md) is descriptive; the selected 37-frame clean segment is excluded from any claim that a rally was sampled independently of tracking success.

## 1. Entire-rally validation: primary next study

Review **S3 P34** first because its 30-fps candidate and gap records already exist. The [focused 17-frame click review](../data/matches/yt_u5HnY2aNW14/screening/s3p34_remaining_rally_review/README.md) completes a systematic 46-frame, 10-Hz whole-interval sample; the full 85-frame view remains optional. Review **S1 P5** second as a contrasting preselected point with a lower six-box candidate rate (63/108). For each point, use source frames to bracket serve contact and the last live contact/first dead-ball frame. Keep an interval if an exact event frame cannot be read, and report the denominator as *source-bracketed candidate interval*, not exact rally duration. Do not choose a replacement rally because it tracks better.

At each source frame, keep six stable player slots. For each slot, record: source-visible identity (`digit_confirmed`, `sequence_consistent`, `uncertain`, `not_visible`); detector match (`same_player`, `wrong_player`, `no_box`, `uncertain`); physical state (`grounded`, `airborne`, `feet_occluded`, `not_visible`, `uncertain`); and whether the grounded box bottom is a plausible floor proxy. **Manual shoe clicks are not required on every rally frame**; they are reserved for the stratified position-error sample below. A visible airborne player may have a valid identity and box while the **floor position is null**. A missing box remains missing even when the player is visible. No held point or interpolation is counted as observed.

The primary denominator is six slots times the source frames in the reviewed interval. Report separate proportions for (a) player visible in the source, (b) candidate box present, (c) candidate matched to the same player after source review, (d) valid physical state known, and (e) grounded court proxy accepted **among slots judged grounded**, with its numerator and denominator. Also report the fraction of frames with six matched boxes and the fraction with six accepted grounded coordinates. The latter is expected to fall during blocks and is not a measure of identity-tracking failure. Show time plots and reasons for every gap. Mark single-reviewer source judgments as such; do not call them independent ground truth.

For position error, use a **frozen stratified frame sample** that includes early, middle, late, overlap, airborne-adjacent and missing-box intervals, selected from source time and state before reading numerical detector errors. On readable grounded players, click shoe contacts in unboxed images, then compare to box-bottom court points. Report missing/manual-unreadable counts and per-frame clustered summaries. Do not infer a population error bound from the observed maximum. Review the court transform with held-out landmarks across near and far depth before treating metre metrics as validated; keep calibration, identity and floor states separate in the output.

The whole-rally result may be a **validated observation timeline with explicit unavailable floor positions** even if no continuous six-grounded metric series exists. The pre-existing strict calculator's zero count remains accurate for its stronger all-six-grounded, formally approved metric gate until that gate is actually met. Do not quietly change that rule or call a source-bracketed candidate interval an exact rally.

## 2. Transfer across matches

Use the already source-selected [Lynx–ProVolley point](../data/matches/yt_AoEP-YiWRvc/pilot/selection.json) and the previously proposed [ProVolley–UTS time-quintile rally r3](../data/matches/yt_FqE5pTJqx8g/pilot/selection.json). These are two **additional recordings**, not yet representative samples of matches. Their source boundaries remain provisional. The fixed technical screen uses the same YOLOv8n person model, confidence 0.20, image size 960, ByteTrack and a 0.2-second grid; it produces [AoEP raw candidates](../data/matches/yt_AoEP-YiWRvc/pilot/transfer_5hz_candidate_screen.json) and [FqE raw candidates](../data/matches/yt_FqE5pTJqx8g/pilot/transfer_5hz_candidate_screen.json). Neither output has a verified near-side team, jersey identities, floor anchors or approved court transform.

Next, confirm source-only serve/end brackets and near-side roster in both recordings, then review the same time-grid frames for all six visible players. Count detector recall against the reviewed six, wrong-team boxes and ID switches. Use a small prespecified early/middle/late grounded shoe-reference sample for each recording and calibrate each stable camera segment separately. Report results **per recording**, with frame and slot denominators and uncertainty/missingness, before any pooled estimate. Different source resolution, frame rate, court view and provisional source selection limit generalisation. The FqE proposal was model-assisted and belongs to the development set; do not label it held-out.

## 3. One volleyball-specific transition measure

The selected metric is **receive-to-first-attack elapsed time** for a point in which the measured near-side team receives the serve. This preserves the earlier decision not to claim a rotation state. A reviewer marks the first near-side serve-reception contact frame and the first subsequent near-side attack-contact frame from unboxed source video. The value is `(attack_contact_frame - reception_contact_frame) / source_fps` seconds. Record which contact is uncertain, whether the attack exists, and a frame interval when the instant is ambiguous. If either event is unobservable, the metric is null. This metric describes a volleyball phase transition; it does not assert that a particular formation threshold was reached or that the transition was effective.

The two additional source intervals have a [frozen event form and clickable source-frame review](../data/processed/receive_to_attack_transition/README.md). Confirm that the measured near-side team actually receives and attacks; if not, record a null. Event annotation must precede viewing any derived transition time. Report at most descriptive point-level values until several independently selected comparable points and outcomes are available. Do not infer a winner effect from one rally or from windows chosen by tracker availability.

## Decision rule for thesis claims

The thesis can report a completed extension only after the relevant human source review and recalculation. Until then, retain the existing prototype results and identify these outputs as protocol, raw candidate screens or partial coverage. The first concrete target is a reviewed, hash-bound **whole S3 P34 observation timeline**, with exact numerators and missingness reasons, even if some floor coordinates remain null.
