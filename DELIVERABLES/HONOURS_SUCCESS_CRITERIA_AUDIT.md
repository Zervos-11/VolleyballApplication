## Interpretation

The original scope is a **research prototype**. It explicitly allows camera, occlusion, identity and dataset limitations if their effects are evaluated and discussed. A measured approximation can be useful; perfect automatic tracking and a significant performance effect are not success criteria.

The first six criteria ask for a prototype method, court-coordinate data, reference evaluation, three calculated metrics, statistical comparison and validity assessment. The repository now has each of those components. A later [S3 P34 joined review](HONOURS_U5_S3P34_REPORTED_LIVE_INTERVAL_RESULT.md) covers all 140 frames of one reviewer-reported live interval with six player-state records per frame and explicit missing or airborne floor positions. This is complete *state-review coverage*, not continuous six-grounded-floor tracking or independent validation; no winner effect has been established. The [full-clip tracklet audit](HONOURS_U5_FULL_CLIP_TRACKLET_AUDIT.md) preserves candidate positions and missingness for the full frozen denominator; its raw IDs are not named players. The eight-window comparison remains availability-selected. Its conditional label fractions must not be reported as confirmatory p-values.

The [final thesis draft](HONOURS_U5_THESIS_FINAL_DRAFT.md) states what the prototype produced, how accurate its selected proxy reference was, where it failed, and what can be concluded about coverage and outcome association. The S1 P6 source-timed diagnostic has five reviewed six-player state samples, four with six grounded floor positions and one with two observed airborne players whose floor coordinates are correctly null. Its [out-of-point court check](HONOURS_U5_S1P6_CALIBRATION_CHECK.md) passed the frozen operational screen, but it does not establish continuous 30-fps floor coordinates or a reliable winner effect. Criterion 7's **writing content** is complete in the draft; submission and academic acceptance are still outstanding. The supervisor must judge whether the limited trajectories satisfy the signed scope.

The completed [S3 P34 30-fps candidate-interval review](HONOURS_U5_S3P34_FULL_30FPS_REVIEW_SCORE.md) now covers all 138 frames between 36001 and 36138 under a single-reviewer, candidate-assisted protocol. It accepts 765/828 grounded box proxies and 93/138 all-six frames. This strengthens criterion 6's missingness and validity analysis. Serve and point-end frames remain bracketed, calibration is not formally approved for strict metrics, and airborne or unboxed positions remain null; the zero strict complete-rally result is unchanged.

The supplementary [transfer check](HONOURS_ELEVATED_TRANSFER_REVIEW_STATUS_2026-11-06.md) reports two completed source-selected points: Pepperdine–USC has 48/48 reviewed five-Hz frames, 14/48 six-grounded-proxy frames and 12/18 selected shoe comparisons (median 0.443 m); USC–BYU has 46/46 reviewed frames, 17/46 six-box frames, no six-grounded-proxy frame under its unresolved floor verdicts and 15/18 selected shoe comparisons (median 0.355 m). Both points were reviewed by one person after detector exposure. Princeton–Long Beach remains partial. These data strengthen the evaluation of protocol portability and missingness but do not change the primary outcome comparison or the strict complete-rally count.

The [source-event extension](HONOURS_RECEIVE_TO_ATTACK_TRANSITION_RESULT.md) provides one receive-to-first-attack interval and one null result because the student reported no near-side attack in the second selected point. The latter verdict was supplied in chat; reception and receive status for that point were not separately specified. A separate [USC–BYU example](HONOURS_USC_BYU_SIDEOUT_EVENT_RESULT.md) records one reviewer-confirmed serve–receive–set–attack-and-win sequence with a 2.200 s reception-to-first-attack time. Its score fields were left blank, it was chosen after inspecting its outcome, and it has no validated six-player floor trajectory. These event examples do not change the primary winner comparison or estimate a side-out rate.
