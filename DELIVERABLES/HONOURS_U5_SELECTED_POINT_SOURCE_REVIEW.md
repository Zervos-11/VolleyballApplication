## Net-side geometry screen

The elevated camera makes the **floor centreline beneath the net** a useful first check. The [net-side screening script](../scripts/screen_u5_net_side.py) applies the staged line at image `y=313` to the saved person proposals. A box whose bottom centre falls above that line is put in `net_boundary_review` and excluded from the automatic near-floor-side count; it is **not** labelled a far-team player. The [machine-readable result](../data/matches/yt_u5HnY2aNW14/screening/net_side_gate_screen.json) records every decision and source hash.

Across the 127 coarse live seconds, 28 boxes need boundary review. Seconds with at least six geometric candidates fall from **94 to 82**. The known false six at 1192 s falls to five because its far-side white box bottom is at `y=307.1`. Of the 17 already visually accepted six-near-player snapshots, **15** still have six after the geometry screen; the near players at **404 and 1137 s** fall just beyond the line (`y=312.7` and `y=308.0`) and must retain their visual-review status. This demonstrates why the image line cannot be a hard team classifier: a blocker or airborne near player can project beyond it. The new counts are screening counts, not validated team positions or eligibility for metre-valued metrics.

The [four full-frame review sheets and numbered crop sheet](../data/matches/yt_u5HnY2aNW14/screening/net_boundary_review/) cover all 28 flagged boxes in 24 sampled seconds. [Single-reviewer side labels](../data/matches/yt_u5HnY2aNW14/screening/net_boundary_adjudication.json) classify **18 as far-side** and **10 as near-side**, including the near players at 404 and 1137 s. Restoring those visually near boundary boxes yields **87/127 seconds with at least six candidate boxes**. This is still only a candidate count: boxes on the near side of the line can contain far-side overlap or duplicates, and no continuous six-player roster or ground-contact accuracy has been established.

## Remaining gates

1. Source-review the exact serve/dead-ball interval or a prespecified live-play window for each frozen point. Keep any point that fails review as an exclusion with its reason.
2. Verify six near-side teammates and stable IDs through each chosen live window. Use the pilot's jump-gap reconnection only where both endpoint identities are supportable; do not invent airborne floor positions.
3. Test the box-bottom ground proxy against an independent small floor-contact reference, including libero and net-action examples, before using metre-valued team metrics.
4. Compute the three prespecified team metrics with eligible-frame fractions and compare winners within sets only if both outcome groups retain enough eligible rallies.
