These are **elevated endline** cameras, not overhead cameras. The short excerpts suggest a consistent view at the checked times; they do not establish that every rally is intact, that there are no cuts or zooms, or that one calibration works throughout each recording. Camera geometry must be calibrated separately for each stable segment. A higher view can make court intersections easier to locate, but a box bottom on an airborne player still cannot be treated as a measured floor position.

## Proposed next source-only step

For Princeton–Long Beach and Pepperdine–USC, inspect the recording without detector overlays and freeze a rally-selection rule and the selected serve/end brackets **before** checking which rally tracks well. Record whether the source is condensed or cut within the selected rally, the near-side team and six-player roster, visible court landmarks, and any camera change. Preserve a failed or unreadable selected rally in the denominator rather than replacing it because detection is difficult. Run the same detector and source-review protocol per recording, and report each recording separately. The existing Concordia–UCLA results remain the primary prototype baseline.

The earlier AoEP and FqE candidates in the [frozen extension protocol](HONOURS_U5_RESEARCH_EXTENSION_PROTOCOL.md) remain developmental source screens. Their raw candidate outputs must not be combined with these recordings as if they were selected under one common rule. The [protocol amendment](HONOURS_U5_TRANSFER_SOURCE_AMENDMENT_2026-11-06.md) records the prospective choice before any new transfer labels.
