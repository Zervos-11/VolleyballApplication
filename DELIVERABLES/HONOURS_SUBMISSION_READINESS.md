## What the results support

- The eight source-screened 0.8-second windows contain 240 approximate court-position proxies and three calculated team-geometry measures. They are a working measurement prototype.
- The earlier [core-only register](HONOURS_U5_SELECTED_CLIP_COURT_COVERAGE.md) preserved 1,005 possible 5 Hz instants, including 370 then-unscreened instants. The later [full-clip pass](HONOURS_U5_FULL_CLIP_TRACKLET_AUDIT.md) screens all 1,005 instants and yields 479 contiguous raw tracklet segments with explicit gaps. A [source spot check](HONOURS_U5_FULL_CLIP_SPOTCHECK.md) finds no obvious transfer at three displayed instants in each of two long runs; white #28 is the near-side libero. It does not fill missing players or certify entire rallies.
- An [every-sample review](HONOURS_U5_FULL_RUN_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md) checks all 216 player crops across those two 18-sample runs and finds the same six jerseys by raw ID at each sampled instant. Two [in-run manual shoe comparisons](HONOURS_U5_FULL_RUN_POSITION_REFERENCE.md) show 0.216 m and 0.523 m box-bottom position error at one frame. The frames between samples and full-rally grounded positions remain unverified.
- A separate [30 fps detector screen](HONOURS_U5_TWO_RUNS_30FPS_AUDIT.md) covers those 206 source frames: six candidates in all 103 frames of one run and 93/103 in the other, with ten temporary missing detections. It does not replace visual jersey review between 5 Hz samples.
- Selected non-anchor manual shoe positions show measurable proxy error; one complete selected team frame gives direct differences for all three geometry measures.
- The within-set-and-phase win/loss comparison has been calculated. It is exploratory and statistically inconclusive. The data do not establish that spacing or coverage changes point outcomes.
