The other set-three point 11 candidate frames also contain overlap, a missing box, or a player on the floor; they do not supply a short, clean six-box trajectory. The other set-two and set-three point 38 proposals likewise do not establish six distinct boxed near-side players. These observations do not change the user's saved annotations.

**Decision:** The targeted detector search found two visually screened six-box snapshots in set-three UTS-won points 11 and 37. They are selected snapshots, not complete rally trajectories or a finished set-stratified winner comparison. A box-centre analysis still needs comparable live intervals and near-side player association across both outcomes. If that is infeasible on this fixed sample, report measured attrition and seek an approved research-scope refinement rather than silently changing the sample or treating detector counts as players.

A separate check of the saved set-three ProVolley-won point 27 at [frame 187701](../data/processed/honours_missing_outcome_box_candidates/s3_p27_f187701_saved.jpg) shows why six saved boxes are not always six near-side players: the box labelled `18` encloses a black-clad far-side player in the net cluster, while the saved team field is `unknown`. This point remains ineligible for the box-centre example until the near-side player is correctly located.
