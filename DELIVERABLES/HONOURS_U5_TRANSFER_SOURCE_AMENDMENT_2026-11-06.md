# Transfer-source protocol amendment — 6 November 2026

The [extension protocol](HONOURS_U5_RESEARCH_EXTENSION_PROTOCOL.md) was frozen before these four additional recordings were supplied. This dated amendment changes **which recordings will be screened for the next transfer study**; it does not revise the original 18-point Concordia–UCLA sample, the S3 P34 review, or any existing result.

Before new detector or player-reference review, choose [Princeton–Long Beach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-siJXMXKcbo) and [Pepperdine–USC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_BOwnLLVaPs) as the two new transfer sources. Their elevated views and different teams make them useful camera and team checks. [UCLA–Grand Canyon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tz106pLf3yY) is a reserve only if a chosen recording lacks an intact, reviewable selected rally; record the reason for substitution. The [dated screen](HONOURS_ADDITIONAL_ELEVATED_SOURCE_SCREEN_2026-11-06.md) documents the limited visual evidence behind this choice.

**Prospective rally rule, frozen before a new detector screen:** in each chosen recording, use the first new serve that begins at or after source time 05:00, regardless of teams, point result, player separation or expected tracking quality. Keep that rally in the denominator if the source has a cut, an unreadable boundary or an unobservable player; record the problem instead of substituting the next rally. If the timestamp falls inside a rally, wait for the next serve. This gives one comparable source-time anchor per recording without using detector availability. A source edit may make the true serve or end ambiguous; preserve interval brackets and label it as a source-bracketed point rather than an exact live rally.

For each chosen source, save the selected point and any source-cut reason. Review serve/end brackets, near-side roster and court landmarks from source frames. Process the selected rally with the same model, settings and 0.2-second grid specified in the frozen protocol, adjusting source frame numbers for its actual fps. Source-review all six slots on that grid; keep airborne floor coordinates and missing boxes null. Use a prespecified early/middle/late grounded shoe-reference sample and a held-out court-landmark check for each camera. Report counts and errors per recording, including unreadable and missing cases.

The AoEP and FqE outputs retain their original developmental status. Do not describe the new elevated recordings as held-out evidence until their rally rule and labels are frozen before detector inspection. The receive-to-first-attack event form remains a separate exploratory analysis; a new event case requires its own source-only selection and bracket record.
