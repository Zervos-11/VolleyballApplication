# S1 P6 court-line mapping check

The [protocol](protocol.json) froze source frames 1759 and 1783, six painted
near-court intersections per frame, the pre-existing refined transform hash,
and the 0.2 m maximum residual rule before new marks were entered. The same
reviewer may use the [local click page](http://127.0.0.1:8041/s1p6-calibration-review).

Choose each numbered intersection and click the centre of the painted lines.
The enlarged crop allows a more precise second click. The **floor net line**
is below the suspended net, near image row 313. If a line crossing cannot be
located, mark it uncertain or occluded and leave the point blank. Save each of
the two frames, then freeze the twelve decisions. No pixel typing is needed.

Because the fixed camera makes the line positions look nearly identical, the
second frame has a **Use first frame as starting points** button. It overlays
the six saved points from frame 1759 on frame 1783. Check each intersection on
the second source image and click its centre to confirm or correct it. Copied
points remain labelled as copied until clicked and cannot be frozen as twelve
separate source-image marks.

The form is a source-only, out-of-point operational check of a transform fitted
at S3 P34. A passing court-line check would not validate airborne floor points,
full 30-fps identity continuity, or a complete fixed-window metric. The prior
staged-transform screen failure remains in the record.
